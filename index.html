Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026 · Simple, honest, short.

📦

What we collect

  • Anonymous session ID — A random ID on your device to remember your likes and saves. No personal info attached.
  • Your likes & saves — Stored locally on your device with your anonymous ID only.
  • Report form — If you report a wallpaper, we collect your name and email to review the report only.
🚫

What we never collect

  • Your real name or email (no login required)
  • Location or GPS data
  • Contacts, camera, or microphone
  • Any browsing data outside this app
  • Device identifiers or IMEI
☁️

Data storage

Your likes and saves are stored locally on your device. Nothing personal is sent to our servers. Wallpaper images are served from secure cloud infrastructure.

SeoulWalls is for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
